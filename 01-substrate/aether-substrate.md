## 2. Gravity as an Inflow Current (Resolving the $G$ Discrepancy)

### The Failure of the Static Constant
High-precision laboratory determinations of the Newtonian gravitational constant ($G$) persistently disagree beyond their reported experimental uncertainties. Mainstream physics attributes this to unaccounted systematic errors because it presumes $G$ is an immutable scalar invariant of empty space.

### The Aether Current Model
In a fluid-dynamic substrate, gravity is not an attractive pull intrinsic to mass, nor is it a passive depression in empty spacetime.
* **The Sink Mechanism:** Stable topological solitons (nuclei, planets, stars) sustain their standing-wave structure by drawing in the surrounding medium. Every concentrated mass acts as an aether sink.
* **Gravitational Acceleration:** What is measured as gravitational acceleration is the hydrodynamic drag and centripetal pressure gradient of the inward-flowing substrate:
  $$\mathbf{g} = -(\mathbf{v}_{\text{aether}} \cdot \nabla)\mathbf{v}_{\text{aether}}$$
* **The Variation in $G$:** Because the local density, shear, and inflow velocity of the medium fluctuate in response to large-scale Birkeland currents and ambient plasma environments, $G$ is an environmental state variable rather than an absolute constant.

## 3. Electromagnetism: Compression vs. Vorticity

In this framework, electromagnetic phenomena are hydrodynamic states of the same medium rather than separate fields requiring independent carrier particles:

* **Electric Fields ($\mathbf{E}$):** Longitudinal acoustic pressure gradients (compression and rarefaction zones of the substrate).
  $$\mathbf{E} \propto \nabla P_{\text{aether}}$$
* **Magnetic Fields ($\mathbf{B}$):** Transverse rotational shear and vorticity currents within the fluid.
  $$\mathbf{B} \propto \nabla \times \mathbf{v}_{\text{aether}}$$
* **Radiation (Light):** Transverse torsional shear waves propagating through the elastic fluid matrix, eliminating the paradox of self-propagating wave-packets traveling through a vacuum.
