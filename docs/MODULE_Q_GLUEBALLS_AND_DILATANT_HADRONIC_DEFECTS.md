## 1. Executive Summary

Standard Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD) treats gluons as fundamental gauge bosons and glueballs as exotic bound states of pure force fields without valence quarks.

In Scalar Relaxation Cosmology (SRC), there are no separate force fields or fundamental point particles. The strong force is the **dilatant hardening** of the scalar substrate ($\phi$)—where effective lattice viscosity ($\gamma$) and stiffness shoot up under extreme strain rates at sub-femtometer scales ($<1\text{ fm}$). 

A glueball (such as the observed $X(2370)$ state) is simply a **localized topological defect dominated by short-range dilatant/shear stress**, rather than a persistent 720° vortex knot ("matter").
