# Circuit Dynamics: The Distributed Power and Self-Limiting Valve Model

## 1. Core Principle: Distributed Micro-Power
Energy in Scalar Relaxation Cosmology (SRC) is not generated by localized point-mass consumption (such as nuclear fuel burnup). It is a **distributed, aggregated trickle** drawn from the dense neutrino substrate sea. 
- A single uncoupled neutrino/antineutrino pair carries an infinitesimal quantum of rotational torque.
- Aggregated across a systemic topological node (a star, a planet, or a galactic core), the volume of interacting substrate produces massive macroscopic power.
- The system is an open-circuit induction engine, not a closed-box thermodynamic decay system.

## 2. The Self-Limiting Valve Mechanism (The Cascade Governor)
To prevent runaway thermal or electrical destruction, the system operates as a **damped, self-regulating plasma oscillator** governed by impedance feedback.

### The Hierarchy of Relief Valves:
1. **The Micro-Trickle (Baseline):** Continuous ambient flux and stellar winds. Maintains nominal charge balance across the interplanetary circuit.
2. **The Intermediate Valve (Solar Cycles):** Periodic 11-year sunspot cycles and coronal mass ejections (CMEs). Vents routine heliospheric over-pressurization.
3. **The Major Valve (The 12ka Reset):** Galactic Current Sheet (GCS) crossings. A heavier structural load that triggers planetary crustal relaxation (scalar spring-back) and magnetic excursions, resetting the local system state without destroying the host body.
4. **The Core Safety Valve (Polar Jets / AGN Bleed):** Continuous synchrotron and jet radiation from extreme high-winding nodes ("black holes"). Vents excess rotational torque along the rotational poles to prevent core-wide structural failure.

## 3. Impedance Feedback (Why the System Doesn't Explode)
When a major valve opens (e.g., a super-CME or a core discharge):
- The sudden energy dump draws down local current density.
- The local impedance spikes, temporarily choking the input "pipe" (Birkeland current channel).
- The potential drop starves the discharge, automatically snapping the valve shut before a catastrophic chain reaction can occur.
- The system returns to baseline equilibrium.

## 5. Macro-Scale Mass Ingestion and Extended Filamentary Feeding
At the galactic tier, external perturbations do not arrive as point-mass impacts. When a star or large mass concentration falls toward the core node, the extreme scalar density gradient ($\nabla \Phi$) exerts differential tension, stringing the infalling body into an extended, drawn-out plasma filament (electrodynamic tidal disruption).

- **The Continuous Fuel Rail:** Rather than triggering an instantaneous explosive shock, the disrupted star becomes a prolonged, high-density current filament. It acts as an auxiliary feeding rail, channeling ionized matter and charge directly into the core's magnetic topology over extended periods.
- **Dynamic Output Adjustment:** The core adapts to this increased mass-energy flux by elevating its internal tension and ramping up its polar jet discharge. The node does not undergo total structural failure; it dynamically adjusts its leakage rate to match the incoming current load.
- **Early-Galaxy Maturation:** This continuous, filamentary feeding mechanism explains the mature, high-mass galaxies observed at extreme redshifts ($z > 14$) by JWST. Galaxies do not wait for slow, stochastic gravitational agglomeration of cold dust; their central engines are continuously powered, structured, and regulated by high-capacity filamentary inflows from the surrounding cosmic web.
