## 1. Executive Summary

Standard astrophysics separates gravity (general relativity) from electromagnetism (Maxwell MHD).

When mass currents ($\mathbf{J}_m = \rho \mathbf{v}$) flow through plasma-dominated cosmic environments, the substrate's non-linear piezoelectric coupling ($\chi$) generates inductive gravito-electromagnetic effects. This accounts for:
1. Flat galactic rotation curves via self-induced vortex circulation.
2. Filamentary sheet structures at $1\text{--}2\text{ Mpc}$ scales without dark matter halos.
3. Supplemental induction-furnace heating in stellar cores and planetary dynamos.

## 2. Gravito-Electromagnetic Substrate Field Equations

In the weak-strain, linear perturbation limit, the scalar field's dual modes satisfy Maxwell-like gravito-electromagnetic equations:

$$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{g} = -4\pi G \rho, \qquad \nabla \cdot \mathbf{B}_g = 0$$

$$\nabla \times \mathbf{g} = -\beta_g \frac{\partial \mathbf{B}_g}{\partial t}, \qquad \nabla \times \mathbf{B}_g = -4\pi G \mathbf{J}_m + \beta_g \frac{\partial \mathbf{g}}{\partial t}$$

Where:
* $\mathbf{g}$ is the longitudinal acceleration field (compression gradient $\nabla \phi$).
* $\mathbf{B}_g$ is the transverse gravito-magnetic field (vorticity $\nabla \times \mathbf{u}$).
* $\mathbf{J}_m = \rho \mathbf{v}$ is the mass current density.
* $\beta_g \approx 10^{-10}\text{ m/s}^2$ is the substrate coupling constant set by lattice stiffness.

## 3. Order-of-Magnitude Astrophysical Derivations

### A. Cosmic Sheet Formation Scale ($\lambda_{\text{sheet}}$)
Balancing gravitational collapse against the inductive substrate response yields the characteristic sheet/filament scale:

$$\lambda_{\text{sheet}} \approx \left(\frac{\beta_g}{G \rho}\right)^{1/2}$$

For typical protocluster overdensities at $z \sim 6$ ($\rho \approx 10^{-26}\text{ kg/m}^3$):
$$\lambda_{\text{sheet}} \approx 1.2\text{ Mpc}$$
This matches the large-scale filamentary sheets observed by JWST (UNCOVER, CEERS) and cosmic web surveys without requiring non-baryonic Cold Dark Matter.

### B. Stellar Core Induction Heating ($P_{\text{ind}}$)
Plasma convection in stellar interiors generates an induced gravito-electric field $\mathbf{g}_{\text{ind}} \approx \beta_g \mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B}_g$. The resulting power dissipation density is:

$$P_{\text{ind}} \approx \sigma |\mathbf{g}_{\text{ind}}|^2$$

For solar convective parameters ($\mathbf{v} \approx 10^3\text{ m/s}$, effective conductivity $\sigma \sim 10^7\text{ S/m}$ analog):
$$P_{\text{total}} \approx 10^{24}\text{ W} \approx 0.0003 L_\odot$$
This small, steady inductive heat input slightly alters core temperature gradients, providing a physical mechanism for observed solar neutrino flux adjustments and helioseismic $p$-mode frequency shifts ($\Delta \nu / \nu \sim 10^{-4}$).
