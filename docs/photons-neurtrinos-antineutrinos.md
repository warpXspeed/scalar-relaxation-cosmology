3.  **Frequency Linkage:** As the dipole rotates about its common center of mass, the angular velocity ($\omega$) is directly proportional to the observed frequency.
    *   Higher frequency = Higher angular velocity ($\omega$).
    *   Higher energy = Higher rotational kinetic energy of the dipole.
