### A. Planetary Core "Missing Heat"
* **Mercury:** A disproportionately large iron core with an active magnetic field despite its small mass. SRC attributes this to strong inductive coupling given its proximity to the Sun's core current density.
* **Io & Outer Moons:** Intense electromagnetic shear between Jupiter's magnetosphere and the solar wind generates inductive core currents, supplementing tidal heating.
* **Earth Geomagnetic Reversals:** SAA expansion and field weakening correspond to regional variations in galactic current input, modulating core convection without relying solely on random internal fluid instabilities.

### B. Rapid High-Redshift Galaxy Formation (JWST Data)
Standard gravitational collapse predicts billions of years to form mature galaxies. Observations at $z > 10$ reveal fully formed galaxies early in cosmic history.
