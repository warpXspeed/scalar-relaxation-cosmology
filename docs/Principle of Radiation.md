## Principle of Radiation-Zone Isotopic Equilibrium

1. Isotopic signatures (e.g., Δ17O, Ti, Cr, noble gases) are functions of the 
   ambient radiation field, photochemical mass-independent fractionation (MIF), 
   and particle flux (Birkeland/SEP streams).
2. Bodies occupying or originating within the same radial heliospheric conduit 
   are subjected to the identical radiation fluence (Φ).
3. Therefore, identical isotopic composition between Earth, the Moon, and inner-system 
   material is the natural consequence of shared radiation-environment exposure, 
   not evidence of a single localized giant-impact event.
