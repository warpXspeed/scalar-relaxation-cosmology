blank# SRC-13: Redshift and the Fallacy of Expanding Space

## Overview
Standard cosmology interprets galactic redshift as evidence of an 'expanding universe' fueled by 'Dark Energy.' The SRC framework redefines redshift as an **intrinsic interaction effect** between light and the Plenum.

1. **The Balloon Analogy:** Mainstream physics treats space like an elastic sheet to preserve the Big Bang singularity. This requires the invention of 'superluminal expansion,' a clear violation of local physical limits.
2. **The SRC Correction:** Redshift is a cumulative energy-loss effect. As wave-packets propagate through the dense, scalar plenum (Aether), they undergo 'scalar relaxation.' The further the light travels, the more frequency it loses. 

## The "Rubber Sheet" Fallacy
- **Standard Model:** Redshift = Velocity (Doppler Effect).
- **SRC Reality:** Redshift = Distance/Substrate Interaction.
- **The Result:** The universe is infinite, stable, and stationary. The 'cosmic horizon' is not a boundary of space, but a boundary of *optical visibility* due to total signal attenuation over extreme distances.

## Law of Parsimony
> **Law of Optical Attenuation:** "A scientific model that requires space to stretch faster than light is a fantasy. Redshift is the signal of a photon's interaction with the plenum, not the motion of a galaxy moving away."
