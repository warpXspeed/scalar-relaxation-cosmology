## 3. Fundamental Entities: Photons vs. Electrons

### A. The Photon (The Wave/Ripple)
*   **Nature:** Pure propagating rotational energy within the lattice bonds. It has no mass; it is a disturbance in the substrate’s potential.
*   **Formation:** Created by stress events (e.g., electron orbital transitions, lattice cracks).
*   **Motion:** Travels via **Sympathetic Hand-off**. Node A rotates $\rightarrow$ induces rotation in Node B $\rightarrow$ Node A relaxes. This is a coherent ripple moving across the honeycomb walls.
*   **Speed ($c$):** Determined by the stiffness and density of the lattice bonds. It is the maximum speed of information transfer through $\phi$.
*   **Redshift:** Cumulative energy loss (viscous damping) as the ripple traverses billions of hand-offs. Frequency drops because energy bleeds into the substrate, not because space expands.

### B. The Electron (The Knot/Particle)
*   **Nature:** A localized, stable toroidal knot of trapped rotational energy. It is a "spalled" fragment of $\phi$ that has collapsed out of wave propagation into a standing structure.
*   **Formation:** **"Forced Joining."** High-energy photons collide or are compressed by lattice stress until they lock into a self-reinforcing loop (torus). This requires an initial EM energy input to overcome the threshold for knot stability.
*   **Motion:** Moves via **Sequential Displacement ("Bumping")**. It cannot hand-off cleanly like light because it is too complex/massive. It physically drags its local distortion through the lattice or hops between atomic potential wells.
    *   *In Conductors:* Slow, diffusive "drunken walk" (~1mm/min) due to scattering off atomic lattices.
    *   *In Vacuum:* Slower than $c$ due to inertia (resistance to lattice shear).
*   **Mass ($m$):** A measure of how tightly the knot is bound and how much it resists displacement by the lattice flow.

## 4. Forces: Drag, Shear, and Flow

### Gravity: The Convergent Flow (The Shepherd)
*   **Mechanism:** Global relaxation of $\phi$. Massive concentrations of knots distort the lattice, creating a "sink." The surrounding lattice flows inward to minimize stress (like water draining).
*   **Effect:** A weak, long-range drag that gathers matter. It does not build atoms; it shepherds existing matter into clusters (stars/galaxies).
*   **Origin of Rotation:** As matter falls inward along asymmetric flow lines, angular momentum is imparted, creating spiral structures.

### Electromagnetism: Local Shear (The Builder)
*   **Mechanism:** High-frequency stress and shear between nearby knots or within the lattice bonds.
*   **Electricity:** Radial compression/tension from stationary or slow-moving charges.
*   **Magnetism:** Torsional shear (twisting) caused by moving/spinning charges dragging the lattice with them.
*   **Strength:** Extremely strong ($10^{36}$x Gravity) because it involves direct, local contact rather than global flow.

## 5. Cosmology: The Pulsating Cycle

There was no single "Big Bang." The universe is in a continuous cycle of **Stress $\rightarrow$ Crack $\rightarrow$ Relaxation**.

1.  **Stress Accumulation:** Over cosmic time, gravitational flow and lattice tension build up in specific regions.
2.  **The Crack (Spallation):** When stress exceeds the yield strength of $\phi$, it "cracks." This releases massive EM energy (light/GRBs) and spalls off fragments as matter (electrons/protons).
3.  **Expulsion:** The shockwave from the crack pushes new matter outward.
4.  **Relaxation (Gravity):** As the lattice settles, gravity pulls the scattered debris back toward the center of the crack.
5.  **Structure Formation:** Gravity shepherds matter into stars and galaxies. EM forces build atoms and chemistry within these clusters.

**Redshift & Expansion:** What we perceive as "expansion" is largely cumulative redshift (energy loss) from light traveling through a relaxing lattice, combined with the receding debris fields from ancient crack events. There is no dark energy pushing space apart; there is only lattice relaxation.
