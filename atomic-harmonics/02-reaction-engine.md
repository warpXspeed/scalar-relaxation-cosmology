### Gate 3: Shear Delta Calculation ($\Delta \tau$)

$$\Delta \tau = \tau_{\text{combined}} - (\tau_1 + \tau_2)$$

- **Endothermic (Forced Reactions):** If $\Delta \tau > 0$, the system requires an external energy input to force the geometric alignment.
