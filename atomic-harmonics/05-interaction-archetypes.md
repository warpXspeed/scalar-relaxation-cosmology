# 05: Interaction Archetypes & Algorithmic Chemistry

Rather than cataloging every single chemical reaction in nature, the Harmonic Aether Framework reduces chemistry to three primary **Interaction Archetypes**. Because reactions are governed by deterministic Grassmannian geometry and shear-delta ($\Delta \tau$) calculations, all bonding falls into these structural categories.

---

### Archetype 1: Covalent Bonding (Phase-Locked Co-Rotation)
* **Mechanism:** Two elements with compatible core frequencies ($f$) and complementary peripheral boundary vectors ($\mathbf{V}$) align their phase angles. 
* **The Result:** They form a shared, stable standing-wave bridge in the aether. The combined geometry lowers the overall system tension ($\Delta \tau < 0$), releasing the difference as thermal/acoustic shockwaves (exothermic reaction). 
* **Example:** Carbon and Hydrogen forming methane ($CH_4$)—the peripheral vortex rings lock into a minimum-energy tetrahedral symmetry because mutual fluid turbulence cancels out.

---

### Archetype 2: Ionic Bonding (Pressure-Gradient Attractor)
* **Mechanism:** Involves elements with extreme aetheric pressure differentials ($\Delta P$). One element acts as an aggressive aether sink (high centripetal suction), while the other easily sheds its outer boundary ring.
* **The Result:** The boundary ring transfers completely, turning the elements into balanced pressure-differential nodes that lock together via macroscopic aetheric suction rather than abstract electrostatic point-charges.
* **Example:** Sodium ($Na$) shedding a peripheral ring to Chlorine ($Cl$). The resulting lattice is a stable spatial pressure grid.

---

### Archetype 3: Noble Gas Isolation (Zero Shear)
* **Mechanism:** Elements where the outer boundary vector ($\mathbf{V}$) fully saturates the nodal capacity and torsional shear drops to absolute zero ($\tau = 0$).
* **The Result:** These elements fail the resonance and topological fit tests with almost all other structures. They cannot generate a negative shear delta ($\Delta \tau < 0$) because their local medium is already at harmonic rest.
* **Example:** Helium, Neon, Argon. They do not react because they require an external energy input to force a geometric fracture.

---
