# 04: Element Mapping & Octave Scaling: Hydrogen to Period 2

To demonstrate how the Harmonic Aether Framework replaces the Standard Model's empirical accounting tricks, we map the first few elements using our geometric tuple:

$$\mathbf{E} = (Gr(k, n), f, \tau, \mathbf{V}, \Delta P)$$

Rather than treating atoms as point-mass collections of protons, neutrons, and electrons, we map them as scale-dependent vortex solitons and standing-wave octaves.

---

### 1. Hydrogen ($H$) — The Fundamental Unit
* **Grassmannian Index ($Gr(k,n)$):** $Gr(1,2)$ — The simplest single-plane subspace constraint. A single primary toroidal vortex.
* **Core Frequency ($f$):** Baseline $f_0$ (the fundamental acoustic pitch of the aetheric sink).
* **Torsional Shear ($\tau$):** Near-zero internal angular stress; highly symmetrical polar-equatorial balance.
* **Outer Boundary Vector ($\mathbf{V}$):** $\mathbf{V}_1$ — A single peripheral vortex ring at the first nodal radius.
* **Aetheric Pressure Gradient ($\Delta P$):** Moderate centripetal sink (acts as an electron-boundary attractor, governing its chemical reactivity).

### 2. Helium ($He$) — The First Harmonic Octave Closure
In the standard table, Helium is forced into the far right because of "valence electrons," but it acts inert like a heavy gas. In our model, Helium is the **closure of the first standing-wave octave**.
* **Grassmannian Index ($Gr(k,n)$):** $Gr(2,4)$ — A nested, double-toroid configuration where the inner core achieves counter-rotating phase stability.
* **Core Frequency ($f$):** $2f_0$ (exactly one octave above Hydrogen).
* **Torsional Shear ($\tau$):** Completely balanced ($\tau = 0$). The counter-rotations of the inner core and outer peripheral rings cancel out all aetheric shear. 
* **Outer Boundary Vector ($\mathbf{V}$):** $\mathbf{V}_2$ — A fully saturated dual-ring peripheral boundary. Because the shear is zero, it refuses to interact with other boundary vectors (hence, a "noble" gas—a dead zone of harmonic rest).

---

### 3. The Jump to Period 2 (Lithium & Carbon)
When we step past Helium to Lithium, the core vortex can no longer contain the frequency within the first radial shell. 
* **Topological Phase Shift:** The structure expands to a larger radial node ($\mathbf{V}_3$), which re-opens the aetheric pressure gradient ($\Delta P$) and makes the atom chemically active again.
* **Carbon ($C$):** Represents a higher-order torsional balancing act within Period 2, where the multi-lobed peripheral vectors ($V_4$) achieve stable tetrahedral symmetry—explaining organic bonding geometry through fluid dynamics rather than hybridized orbital probabilities.
