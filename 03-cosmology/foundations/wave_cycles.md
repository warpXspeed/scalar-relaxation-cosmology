# Foundations: Wave Mechanics and the Steady-State Equilibrium

## 1. Abstract
This project replaces the singular, inflationary "Big Bang" with a model of continuous, periodic wave propagation. The universe is a steady-state system characterized by a global "operating rhythm" rather than a finite chronological expansion.

## 2. The Endless Wave Cycle
The universe is an ongoing process of substrate relaxation. Creation is not an event that happened "at the beginning"; it is the continuous propagation of wave-fronts and phase transitions through the $\phi$-lattice.
- **Cycle Periodicity:** The structural evolution of galaxies and solar systems follows a "limit cycle." The system exists in a state of dynamic metastability, oscillating between energy storage (capacitive phase) and energy discharge (reset phase).
- **Infinite Continuity:** There is no absolute beginning and no absolute end. The universe is a self-perpetuating pulse—a cosmic heartbeat—that maintains its structure through constant turnover.

## 3. The Cosmic Background (Thermal Baseline)
The Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) is not a relic of a primordial explosion. It is the **ambient operating temperature of the substrate itself.**
- **The Thermal Equilibrium:** The 2.725 K background represents the steady-state thermalization of the vacuum condensate.
- **Energy Accounting:** As starlight and plasma discharge propagate across the cosmos, they lose energy via the viscous attenuation defined in `substrate_dynamics.md`. This energy is not lost; it is dumped into the phonon bath of the substrate, establishing the observable microwave baseline. 

## 4. Stability vs. Entropy
Mainstream physics predicts a "Heat Death" based on the assumption of a closed, isolated system. In SRC:
- **Open Circuit Dynamics:** Because the system is an open, galactic-scale circuit, it is continuously replenished by the ambient potential of the galactic filamentary web.
- **Equilibrium:** The universe maintains structural integrity because the inflow of galactic current ($J$) is matched by the dissipative relaxation of the substrate. This ensures a permanent, non-entropic state of existence.

## 5. Conclusion
The universe is a static, infinite, and Euclidean manifold. It does not evolve from a singular point, but maintains its structure through the recursive interaction of wave-fronts and substrate currents. We are observers of a permanent, living circuit that requires no "creation event" to justify its present state.
