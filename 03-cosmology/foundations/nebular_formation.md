# Foundations: The Nebula Workshop (Structural Formation)

## 1. The Z-Pinch Assembly Line
Nebulae are not nurseries of gas; they are active plasma discharge workshops. Structure is formed by the **Z-pinch ($\mathbf{J} \times \mathbf{B}$)**, where Birkeland current filaments compress plasma and dust into dense nodes.

## 2. Solid-Core Synthesis
Matter is not formed by slow gravitational drift. The high-current pinch forces heavy-element synthesis (Fe, C, O) during the initial condensation phase. Planets and stars form as **dense, solid slag-cores** accumulated at the nodes of the current rails.

## 3. The Anode-Node Star
Stars are not gas balls; they are solid, conductive anode-nodes. The "fusion" light is an electrical surface-sheath discharge. The structural integrity of the star is provided by a metallic core, confirmed by the presence of coronal holes (windows to the core) and CME heavy-element ejection.
