# Foundations: Unified Origins of Nodes and Filaments

## 1. Abstract
In Scalar Relaxation Cosmology (SRC), there is no separation between the "particle" and the "field." Atoms, celestial bodies, and galactic structures are manifestations of the same topological phenomenon: stable, nested vortices (knots) in the substrate ($\phi$). This page defines the scaling hierarchy from the atomic-scale node to the galactic-scale circuit.

## 2. The Scaling Hierarchy (Bit-to-Node)
All matter is a localized phase-transition in the substrate. The hierarchy is governed by the recursive interlocking of toroidal field geometries:
- **Atomic Nodes:** The primary harmonic of substrate resonance. Atomic bonds are the sympathetic resonance of these interlocking field geometries, replacing the "valence electron" model with field-geometry constraint.
- **Planetary/Stellar Nodes:** Macro-scale armatures. These are high-density accumulations of heavy elements ("slag") forged by the Z-pinch ($\mathbf{J} \times \mathbf{B}$) forces within galactic current filaments.

## 3. Forces as Substrate Tension
We reject the four-force paradigm (Gravity, EM, Strong, Weak). Forces are simply the gradient-pressure of the substrate:
- **Electromagnetism (Active State):** Kinetic shear and current flow within the $\phi$-lattice.
- **Gravity (Relaxed State):** The residual pressure gradient/tension left in the substrate after matter formation. Gravity is the "cleanup crew"—the lagging, non-linear relaxation of the field toward a lower-potential configuration.

## 4. The Unified Energy Source
Matter does not create its own gravitational or electromagnetic potential. It is an **active terminal** in an infinite galactic circuit.
- **The Galactic Birkeland Current ($J_{\text{ext}}$):** The primary driver of all stellar and planetary activity. 
- **The Transduction Process:** Stars (Anodes) and Planets (Inductive Armatures) do not possess "internal furnaces." They are hardware components that convert external ambient galactic current into localized radiative and kinetic work.

## 5. Conclusion
By identifying all matter as "nodes" in a unified circuit, we eliminate the need for distinct "force carriers" (gravitons, photons, gluons). Matter is the medium's state, and Force is the medium's flux.
